      How to play

      There is one secret English word each day. You get no clues — not its length, first letter, or category.

      Type any word. You'll see how close its meaning is to the secret word, from 0 to 100.

      • Freezing 0–19 · unrelated
      • Cold 20–44
      • Warm 45–64
      • Hot 65–84
      • Burning 85–99 · very close

      The score measures semantic closeness from a language model — how often words appear in similar contexts. It isn't a probability or a dictionary rank, and opposites can score high (hot and cold are used in similar places). Only the exact word scores 100.

      Unlimited guesses. After 50, you can ask for a hint — or keep going without one. A new word arrives every day at midnight UTC.

      We count plays anonymously (a random ID kept on your device, no personal data) to improve the game.
